## L1.1
The kernel check is one deterministic tactic application, cheap
and infallible. Policy training needs a corpus of formalized
theorems and many search episodes. Verification is cheap,
learning what to try is dear.

## L2.1
Scale the proposer first. The engine is already certain: every
proposed construction gets a correct verdict. The bottleneck is
the hit rate (0.40), and only better proposals raise it.

## L3.1
It confuses search with proof: 9 of the 13 nodes were dead ends,
and the proof is the 4-node accepted path, not the 13-node
search.

## L4.1
The dream got harder (randomized), so the sim number should
fall. What matters is the real number, which rose from 0.70 to
0.76, and the gap, which narrowed from 0.25 to 0.12. A falling
sim score with a rising real score is the trade working.
