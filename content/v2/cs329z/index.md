Building agentic systems: tools, memory, retrieval, reasoning, and evaluation. Each lesson is a textbook chapter: a concrete problem, a hand-worked toy, the numbers behind every failure, the fix mapped back by name.

## Why this course exists

A randomized trial of 52 engineers learning a new Python library, with versus without AI assistance: the AI users scored 17% lower on the follow-up quiz, about two letter grades, with the gap most pronounced on debugging, the skill seniors are made of (Shen and Tamkin, Anthropic, 2026). The lecture's moral: use AI to learn how to learn. This course teaches you to build the systems behind that result, and to understand what they cost the people who use them.

## Lessons

1. [Lecture 1A: What an Agent Is, and the Loop It Runs](l01-intro-agentic-systems.html), perceive, decide, act, observe, check. ReAct traced turn by turn.
2. [Lecture 1B: Compound AI Systems](l02-compound-ai-systems.html), tool calling done right, MCP, memory, workflows versus agents.
3. [Lecture 2A: LLMs for Builders](l03-llms-for-builders.html), the sampling math and the cost model behind every agent step.
4. [Lecture 2B: Inference-Time Scaling, Structured I/O, Context Engineering](l04-reasoning-and-context.html), spend compute per query, force the output shape, engineer the context.
5. [Lecture 3A: RAG, Indexing, and Chunking](l05-rag-pipeline.html), evidence at answer time, from retriever-reader to RAPTOR.
6. [Lecture 3B: BM25, Dense Retrieval, and the Retriever Zoo](l06-retrieval-methods.html), every retriever priced in milliseconds.
7. [Lecture 3C: Retrieval Inside the Loop and Nine Failure Modes](l07-agentic-retrieval-failure-modes.html), agentic retrieval, each failure with numbers.
8. [Lecture 4: Evaluating Agents with Numbers](l08-evaluating-agents.html), the demo works once. Evaluation covers the other nine runs.
