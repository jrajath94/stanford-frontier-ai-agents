## Honesty note

Nine-week official materials list anchors six teaching units.
Economics content must not be reclassified as agent implementation,
speaker forecasts require evidence labels.

Honesty rules in force:

- The nine-session materials list at
  https://mse435.stanford.edu/materials.html anchors the six teaching
  units. The list was text-fetched on 2026-10-07. Lecture recordings,
  slide decks, linked readings, and transcripts were NOT inspected.
  That extraction remains a subsequent artifact task. Every leaf
  therefore carries claim class OFFICIAL-SCHEDULE (topic named on the
  materials page), REQUESTED-BRANCH (requested prerequisite or
  implementation depth), or OFFICIAL-SOURCE (inspected artifact), and
  status PLANNED / SOURCE ATTRIBUTION PENDING until an inspected
  artifact verifies it.
- No leaf is called "taught" because it appears in a prompt or
  inventory. A leaf closes only when it is taught in a lesson,
  assessed in an exercise, lab, or interview bank, and recorded in
  `coverage_matrix.md` with evidence.
- Numbers in lessons are computed by committed scripts on this machine
  (Python stdlib, numpy, matplotlib) or marked "Not in source". No
  benchmark, speedup, or test result is invented.
- Assignment learning objectives are drawn from the public materials
  list only. All practice is original equivalent work. No assessed
  work is solved. Any claim attributed to a guest speaker carries an
  evidence label and stays SOURCE ATTRIBUTION PENDING until the
  recording or linked reading is inspected.
- The economics/engineering boundary is strict. Supply, demand, cost
  structure, and market claims are taught as economics. They are never
  presented as agent implementation or system design.

## Directory layout

- `README.md` , this file.
- `state.md` , build state and RUN checkpoints.
- `source_manifest.md` , source register with inspection boundaries.
- `source_gaps.md` , unresolved sources and missing artifacts.
- `course_map.md` , nine sessions mapped to six units, with claim
  classes.
- `index.md` , learner navigation.
- `prerequisites.md` , unit prerequisites, local remediation, diagnostic.
- `notation_and_shapes.md` , shared symbols, shapes, units.
- `glossary.md` , terms with one-meaning definitions.
- `currentness.md` , baseline dates and update policy.
- `coverage_matrix.md` , 72 concept rows (U01-U06) with status and
  evidence. All 72 TAUGHT+ASSESSED.
- `visual_audit.md` , figure audit rows per unit.
- `mastery_ledger.md` , per-concept learner mastery state (initial:
  untested).
- `errors.md` , error log for the build and for learner traps.
- `role_gap_map.md` , role rubrics versus unit coverage.
- `lessons/` , one lesson file per unit, 12 concepts each, 15-item
  contract.
- `keys/` , answer keys for lesson assessments, kept separate from
  lessons.
- `labs/` , lab task files, execution-verified runners, and lab keys.
- `interview/` , interview question banks and keys, kept separate.
- `visuals/` , render scripts and audited figures.
- `capstones/` , the two course capstones: research replication
  (runnable `cap01_run.py`, negative results reported honestly)
  and the applied/FDE case (runnable `cap02_run.py`,
  HYPOTHETICAL numbers), each with computed figures.
- `crash-course.md` , all 72 concepts condensed.
- `cheatsheet.md` , formulas, decision rules, numbers.
