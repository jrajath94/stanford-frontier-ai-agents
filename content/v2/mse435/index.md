## What this course is

AI is the biggest technology supercycle since the PC,
the internet, and mobile. A **supercycle** is a long
wave of investment set off by a general-purpose
technology. MS&E 435 unpacks the economics at each
layer of the AI stack, one practitioner per week, each
with a vantage point no textbook can give.

The instructor is Apoorv Agrawal, a Stanford adjunct
lecturer and partner at Altimeter Capital. The course
ran Thursdays in Spring 2026. This course site covers
three of the guest sessions in full: the data-center
builder, the model post-trainer, and the agent
infrastructure CEO. [uncertain: the full course had
roughly nine sessions. transcripts exist here for
three.]

## The one long argument

The six lessons read as one argument, in order.

**Why is anyone spending $650 billion?**
[L01](l01-electrons-to-tokens.html) builds the
demand thesis from the Cobb-Douglas growth equation:
GDP grows from labor, capital, and technology, and AI
is the first technology that makes the labor term
investable. Agents doing real work are **digital
labor**, and the CapEx buys workers, not tools.

**What does the money buy?**
[L02](l02-sixty-million-megawatt.html) opens the
dollar. About $20M per megawatt builds the factory
(power plant, building, labor), $40M fills it with
machines (three quarters GPUs), $60B per gigawatt
total. Renting chips pays back in about four years.
Selling tokens pays back in about two.

**Why do the machines keep getting more valuable?**
[L03](l03-how-models-get-smarter.html) tours the model
layer as a moving bottleneck: AlexNet, the
transformer, pre-training, scaling laws, RLHF,
reasoning models. Each era broke one constraint.

**How does that value reach a business?**
[L04](l04-evals-rlvr-enterprise.html) covers evals,
the 5-percent post-training budget, and the
specialization layer. General models set the floor.
Your evals set the ceiling.

**What does it do to software?**
[L05](l05-software-is-dead.html) is the SaaS
reckoning. Coding agents explode the TAM, deploying
beats writing, and the presentation layer goes
plastic while the system of record holds.

**Where does the value land?**
[L06](l06-where-value-accrues.html) answers the
course's core question. In 2026: below the model.
The token economy's new primitives are the AI
gateway (a CDN for tokens) and the sandbox (EC2 for
agents).

## How to use this course

Read L01 through L06 in order the first time. Each
lesson is self-contained, with hand-worked numbers,
interview Q&A, and a recap. The
[cheatsheet](cheatsheet.html) compresses every lesson
to its numbers. The [crash
course](crash-course.html) retells the arc at
interview speed.
