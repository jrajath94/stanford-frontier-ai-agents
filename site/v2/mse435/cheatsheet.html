Watch-outs

  • The pilot average (61.50) is not the steady-state rate. Never quote pilot cost as the unit price.
  • Analytic hit-rate models overstate small caches by ~12 percent (capstone 1, H1 FAIL). Measure h.
  • Doubling cache 500 to 1,000 gains ~5 points: diminishing, not linear.
  • Moving Y or D after the pilot voids the decision.
  • Build-only business cases understate cost 3-4x.
  • The softest inputs: adoption ramp, success rate, review labor. Instrument all three before investing.
  • Speaker forecasts are [SPEAKER CLAIM, EVIDENCE PENDING]. Never build on an unlabeled claim.
